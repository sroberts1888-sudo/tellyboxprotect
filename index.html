Security architecture

Your VPN identity starts on your device.

TellyBox Protect separates the Android device identity from the server configuration. The backend receives the public key it needs to configure the VPN peer; the private key remains local to the device.

Frequently asked questions

Is TellyBox Protect available to download yet?

Not publicly yet. The app is currently in final release preparation and testing.

Where is my VPN private key stored?

The client private key is generated and kept on the Android device. The backend receives only the corresponding public key needed to configure the service.

Can every website or streaming service be guaranteed to work?

No. Third-party services may apply their own location, security or VPN restrictions, so access to every service cannot be guaranteed.
