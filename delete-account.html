Delete Your Account & Data

You can request deletion of your TellyBox Protect account and associated account data at any time.

How to request deletion

Email support@tellyboxprotect.co.uk from the email address linked to your TellyBox Protect account.

Use the subject TellyBox Protect account deletion request and include the account email address you want deleted.

What will be deleted

Once we verify the request, we will delete or remove the account information and service records associated with the account where applicable, including:

  • Account record and email address
  • Registered VPN device records associated with the account
  • Stored VPN public-key and tunnel configuration records associated with those devices

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained only where reasonably necessary for security, fraud or abuse prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or to maintain the integrity of service records. Any retained information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

VPN private keys

Your client VPN private key is generated and stored on your device and is not stored by the TellyBox Protect backend. Removing the app or clearing its local data removes local app data from that device according to Android's storage behaviour.

Need help?

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact support@tellyboxprotect.co.uk.
