Support & Troubleshooting

If TellyBox Protect is not connecting as expected, try these steps first.

Quick checks

  1. Confirm your normal internet connection is working.
  2. Make sure Android VPN permission has been granted.
  3. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN.
  4. Close and reopen the app.
  5. Restart the device if the problem continues.

Diagnostics

If support asks you to open Diagnostics, it may show information such as:

  • Connection or tunnel state
  • Server endpoint
  • Assigned VPN / tunnel IP address
  • Public keys
  • DNS information
  • App version and build information

Important security note

Never send support your password, VPN private key, access token, or other secret credentials. Public keys and non-secret Diagnostics information may be requested when troubleshooting.
